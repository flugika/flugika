## About me

I'm a Full-Stack Developer from Thailand with a strong visual and creative background. I enjoy taking a scattered idea, planning it, designing the experience, and building it into a working product.

My interest in computers started in childhood, but I first learned real programming at university (B.Eng. Computer Engineering, Suranaree University of Technology). Since then I've worked on an enterprise B2B platform at TCC Technology, delivered freelance projects end to end, and keep building my own products in my free time.

I also draw, play guitar, write, solve Rubik's Cubes, and stream games. That mix shapes how I think about UX/UI and product details.
